# ARI's hummingbird: image prompts

Eight photos of the same hummingbird, one per pose. Make them all in **one ChatGPT
conversation**, in this order, so the bird stays the same. Save each with the file name
shown into a folder on your Desktop called **ARI Pet**, then tell me they're there.

Any size works; square is best. The plain background gets cut out automatically. The
file names match the dog's on purpose: each is the moment the pet shows, not the pose.

## 1. Start with this (sit.png)

> Photorealistic wildlife studio photograph of a single Anna's hummingbird, an adult
> male: iridescent emerald-green back, a brilliant magenta-rose throat and crown that
> catch the light, gray-white chest, slim straight black bill, small dark bright eyes.
> Hovering in place, facing slightly toward the camera, body level, wings caught mid-beat
> with a natural slight motion blur at the tips, tail feathers fanned a little. Soft,
> even studio light from the front left, sharp focus on the eye and throat, detailed
> feathers. No flowers, no feeder, no branch, no props, no text. The whole bird in frame
> with space around it, centered, on a plain seamless light gray background. Square
> image.

## 2 to 8: in the same conversation

Start each one with: **"Same hummingbird, same lighting, same camera, same plain light
gray background, whole bird in frame, no props, square image. Pose:"** and then the pose.

| File | When ARI shows it | Pose |
|---|---|---|
| panting.png | thinking | hovering, leaning forward with its bill pointed slightly down, intent and focused, wings a fast blur |
| alert.png | something needs you | hovering upright, head raised and turned to look straight at the camera, throat flared bright magenta, wings spread wide |
| asleep.png | resting, Mac asleep | perched on nothing, body fluffed round, head tucked down, eyes closed, wings folded, bill pointing slightly up (as hummingbirds sleep) |
| sad.png | out of credits | perched, feathers slightly fluffed, wings folded, head lowered, looking down, subdued |
| lifted.png | dragged upward | rising straight up, wings swept high above the body, tail spread, looking up |
| leap.png | dragged sideways | darting fast to the right in side profile, body stretched long and level, wings swept back, bill pointing right |
| startled.png | dragged downward | braking in mid-air, body tilted back, wings flared forward, tail fanned wide, eyes wide |

"Perched on nothing" matters: anything it sits on would get cut out with it.

If a result shows a different-looking bird, say "That's a different hummingbird. Make it
the same bird as the first image" and try again.
