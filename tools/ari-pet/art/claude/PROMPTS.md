# Claude's golden retriever: image prompts

Eight photos of the same dog, one per pose. Make them all in **one ChatGPT
conversation**, in this order, so the dog stays the same. Save each with the file name
shown into a folder on your Desktop called **Claude Pet**, then tell me they're there.

Any size works; square is best. The plain background gets cut out automatically.

## 1. Start with this (sit.png)

> Photorealistic studio photograph of a cute, athletic young golden retriever, about two
> years old: lean and muscular with a deep chest and well-defined legs, a medium-gold coat
> with lighter cream feathering on the chest, legs, and tail, friendly dark brown eyes,
> a black nose, and soft feathered ears set at eye level. Clean, well-groomed coat. No
> collar, no props, no text. Soft, even studio light from the front left, camera at the
> dog's eye level with a 50 mm lens, sharp focus and detailed fur. The whole dog in
> frame with space around it, centered, on a plain seamless light gray background.
> Square image.
>
> Pose: sitting upright facing the camera, mouth closed, calm and attentive.

## 2 to 8: in the same conversation

Start each one with: **"Same dog, same lighting, same camera, same plain light gray
background, whole dog in frame, square image. Pose:"** and then the pose.

| File | Pose |
|---|---|
| panting.png | sitting facing the camera, mouth open, tongue out, happy panting, head tilted slightly |
| alert.png | standing on all fours, turned slightly toward the camera, head up, ears perked forward, tail raised, eager |
| asleep.png | lying down curled on its side, eyes closed, chin resting on its front paws |
| sad.png | lying down facing the camera, chin on its paws, eyes open looking up, ears drooped, sad puppy eyes |

| leap.png | mid-leap in side profile running to the right, ears flying back, legs stretched out, joyful |
| startled.png | facing the camera, crouched and bracing, ears raised and flipped up, eyes wide, surprised |

If a result shows a different-looking dog, say "That's a different dog. Make it the same
dog as the first image" and try again.
